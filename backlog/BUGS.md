# MeMomy — Bugs

Format per bug:
- **BUG-###** · Short title
- **Status:** `[OPEN]` | `[IN PROGRESS]` | `[FIXED]` | `[WONT FIX]`
- **Severity:** `CRITICAL` (data loss / security) | `HIGH` (feature broken) | `MEDIUM` (degraded) | `LOW` (cosmetic)
- **Epic:** which epic this belongs to
- **Description:** what is happening vs. what should happen
- **Steps to reproduce**
- **Fix notes** (filled in when resolved)
